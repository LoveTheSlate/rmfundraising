Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

This policy covers rmfundraising.com, the personal professional website of Rob Muniak, a fundraising coach with Gold Athletics. It is not the official Gold Athletics website. For Gold Athletics' own policies, visit goldathletics.com.

What this site collects

This site has no contact forms, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. It does not set cookies and does not load tracking scripts. Fonts and images are served from this site itself.

When you contact me

If you call, text or email me using the links on this site, I receive whatever you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address, school or program, and the details of your message. I use that information only to respond to you and to help your program with fundraising through Gold Athletics. I don't sell your information.

If we move forward with a fundraiser, some of your details may be shared with Gold Athletics so the fundraiser can be set up and supported. Gold Athletics handles that information under its own privacy practices.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical information such as your IP address when you visit, for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Links to other sites

This site links to goldathletics.com and other websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and I'm not responsible for how they handle information.

Questions or requests

To ask a question, or to ask me to update or delete information you've shared with me, email rob@rmfundraising.com or call or text 440-821-8333.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
